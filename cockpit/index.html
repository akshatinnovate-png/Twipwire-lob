WHY MONITOR R TESTS ORTHOGONALITY, NOT |RESIDUAL|⊠
A least-squares forecaster f that is still right satisfies E[(y − f)·f] = 0. When the book's meaning flips, errors line up against the forecast and the mean becomes −2E[s²]. The |residual| only grows by a factor of about 1+3R². On real LOBSTER data R² ≈ 1–2 %, so |residual| has almost no power. On injected real-data shifts the |residual| monitor (our Round-1 design) detected – of breaks, while the orthogonality monitor detected –. We changed the design because the data said so.
